What it does

Plan the week, swap what you don't want, shop once.

A week that fits the budget

Pick your cuisines, your supermarkets, the size of your household and a weekly budget in riyals. The scale weighs the expected basket against the budget while you move the slider.

  • 18 cuisines, or a mix
  • Carrefour, Panda, Othaim, Danube, Tamimi and LuLu
  • Cooking days, meals per day and leftovers

Change any meal in one tap

Every meal can be swapped. The three quick options are ranked by what your basket already covers, and each one shows how the basket price changes.

  • Filtered by allergies and appliances
  • Ban a dish and it never comes back
  • Goals: balanced, high protein, cutting or bulking

A list sorted the way you walk the store

The grocery list is grouped by aisle and can split by store. Ticked items move into "في مطبخي", the kitchen view, and cooked meals take their ingredients out of it.

  • Copy the list, sorted by aisle
  • Send it to HungerStation, Nana, Ninja, Keeta, ToYou, Jahez or Mrsool
  • Leftovers count toward next week

Mizan in action

Details

Built for Saudi kitchens and Saudi prices.

مِيزان

Arabic first

The whole app is written in Arabic and laid out right to left, with Thmanyah type on an 8pt grid.

No account

Your profile and plans are saved on the phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Works offline

Planning, swapping and the grocery list all run on the device.

163 halal recipes

Across 18 cuisines and about 128 ingredients, each with macros and a cost per serving in riyals.

Stays on budget

When the week runs over, the planner swaps in cheaper meals in stages, keeping to your chosen cuisines first.

Light and dark

Automatic, light or dark, set from the profile.

Calm motion

Fades between 120 and 260 ms with an ease-out. Nothing slides or bounces.

21 tests

Jest tests cover the catalog, the planner rules and full flows on Android.

How it plans

Four steps, all on the phone.

01

Filter

Recipes that clash with your allergies, appliances, goal or banned dishes are removed first.

02

Shuffle

A seeded shuffle picks the week, so two weeks rarely look the same.

03

Balance

If the basket runs over budget, cheaper meals replace expensive ones in stages.

04

Shop

Ingredients are priced per pack and grouped by aisle and store.
